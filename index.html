About Me

I am a Computer Science Engineering student with a strong curiosity for technology and a desire to continuously improve my technical and professional skills. My journey in computer science has allowed me to explore different areas including programming, software development, databases, web technologies, computer networks, cybersecurity, and Artificial Intelligence.

I enjoy understanding how technology works and converting theoretical concepts into practical applications. Rather than limiting myself to a single technology, I like exploring different areas of computer science and understanding how they can be combined to solve real-world problems.

Throughout my academic journey, I have worked on several projects that helped me gain hands-on experience. My projects include AgriBridge, an agricultural marketplace platform; Student Record Management system in Java; a Secure Hybrid Data Center & Cloud Network using Cisco Packet Tracer; MyTasks, a productivity app; and Arogya Jagruthi, a health awareness platform for rural communities.

I am actively exploring Artificial Intelligence through my involvement in my college AI Club and am a former NSS member. My goal is to start my career as a software developer, gain strong industry experience, and contribute to meaningful technology solutions.
